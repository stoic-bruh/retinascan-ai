# Demo Video

The demonstration video for **RetinaScan AI** showcases the end-to-end clinical workflow, automated image preprocessing, 5-stage Diabetic Retinopathy classification, and Grad-CAM explainability heatmaps.

## Video Deliverable

- **Repository Video File:** [Watch Demo Video (`demo.mp4`)](../demo.mp4)
- **Live Interactive Demo:** [https://retinascan-ai-73ss.onrender.com](https://retinascan-ai-73ss.onrender.com)

## What the Demo Shows

1. **Problem Context:** Need for accessible, trust-centered Diabetic Retinopathy screening in rural settings.
2. **Preprocessing Pipeline:** CLAHE contrast enhancement and circular cropping of digital fundus photography.
3. **Automated Severity Staging:** EfficientNet-B0 prediction across ICDR 5 stages (No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Proliferative) with confidence scoring.
4. **Explainable AI (Grad-CAM):** Dynamic saliency overlays highlighting exact pathological lesion sites (microaneurysms, hemorrhages, hard exudates) with an interactive opacity blend slider.
5. **Actionable Clinical Recommendation:** Standardized triage advice based on disease severity.
